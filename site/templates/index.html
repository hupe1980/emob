Open source · Rust · OCPP 2.1 · OCPI 2.3.0 · Eichrecht · AFIR

Charging software that can prove every kilowatt-hour.

emob is the open-source back office for EV charging in Europe — charge point operator and e-mobility provider in one Rust workspace. It verifies signed meter values under German calibration law, prices and settles them to the cent, and models AFIR, Eichrecht and NIS2 as cited, dated code.
